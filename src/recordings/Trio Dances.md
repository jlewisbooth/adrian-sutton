This is a little suite of three dances for string trio that, in a sense, picks up from where Adrian's violin/viola duo Spring Masque (2012) left off.

## Galliard

Ever since writing the score for the National Theatre’s production of The Revenger’s Tragedy in 2008, Adrian had fallen in love with a certain kind of Renaissance-inspired sound which infuses the first of these dances. The opening characteristic stress pattern of the 6/8 Galliard metre is used as a platform throughout the movement to explore the thematic material in a series of increasingly frequent modulations.</p>

## Sarabande

This is based on a simple tune that Adrian wrote many years ago and until now, never found a home for. Adrian has previously noted how composition frequently works like that.. ideas get tucked away in a drawer, waiting for their right moment. Each of the three instruments gets a turn with the melodic material.

## Rigaudon

An earthy, robust dance in 2/4 time to round the suite off.. though containing some contrapuntal passage work to give the players a workout!
